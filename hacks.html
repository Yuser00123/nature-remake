Some Useful Nature Life Hacks

  • Reuse kitchen water for plants: Use the water you used to wash fruits and vegetables and also the water which comes as a waste from water purifier to water the plants.
  • Biodegrade kitchen waste: You can use kitchen waste like vegetable peels, ruit peels, egg shells to make compost for your garden. This will make the soil nutrient rich.
  • Harvest rainwater: You can place a big bucket or a barallel outside your home during rain to collect fresh water. Rain water is the purest form of water.
  • Use natural light and airflow: Open curtains and windows during the day to get some fresh air and natural light. Use cross ventilations to keep your house cool.
  • Reuse cooled cooking water: Save the nutrient-rich water left over from boiling potatoes, pastas or any other vegetable and then cool the water then give it to your garden plants.
