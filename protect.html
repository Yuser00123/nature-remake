Ways to Protect Our Nature

  • Plant Trees: Trees give clean air, produce oxygen and also are a reason behind rain. They also provide habitat to animals.
  • Save Water: Water is a very orecious resource. Turn off taps when not in use and also fix any leaks.
  • Save Electricity: Turn off fans, lights and other appliances which are not in use.
  • Use Public Transport: Go by cycle or by walk if you want to go to nearby place otherwise use public transportation like bus etc.
  • Don't Litter: Don't throw garbage on the road or any public place. Throw garbage only in dustbins. This helps keep our environment clean and green.
