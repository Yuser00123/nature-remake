Interesting Facts about Nature

  1. Trees can talk to each other: Through underground fungi network, they share nutrients and even warn each other about insect attacking.
  2. Bananas are berries but strawberries are not: Scientifically, a banana fits the definition of a berry, but a strawberry doens't.
  3. Honey never expires: Archaelogists found 3000 years old honey in Egyptian ombs and it was still edible.
  4. Octopuses have 3 hearts: Two hearts pump blood to the gills and the other pumps to the rest of the body.
  5. There are more trees on Earth than stars in the Milky Way galaxy: There are around 3 trillion trees on Earth and 100-400 billion stars in Milky Way.
  6. Butterflies taste with their feets: They stand on a leaf to check if it is good for laying eggs.
  7. The Amazon rainforest makes its own rain: About 50% of its rain is created by itself.
  8. Sharks existed before trees: Sharks are about 450 million years old and trees are about 390 million years old.
